Calculus Kinematics

• Velocity: $v(t) = \frac{dx}{dt} = x'(t)$

• Acceleration: $a(t) = \frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{d^2x}{dt^2} = v'(t)$

• Displacement: $\Delta x = \int_{t_1}^{t_2} v(t) \, dt = x(t_2) - x(t_1)$

Constant Acceleration Equations

• $v = v_0 + at$

• $x = x_0 + v_0 t + \frac{1}{2} a t^2$

• $v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a(x - x_0)$

• $\Delta x = \frac{v_0 + v}{2} \cdot t$

Speeding Up vs. Slowing Down

• Speeding Up: Velocity and acceleration have the SAME sign (both positive or both negative).

• Slowing Down: Velocity and acceleration have OPPOSITE signs (one positive, one negative).

Graph Properties

• Slope of $x(t)$: Velocity $v(t)$

• Concavity of $x(t)$: Acceleration sign ($\cup \implies a>0$, $\cap \implies a<0$)

• Slope of $v(t)$: Acceleration $a(t)$

• Signed Area under $v(t)$: Displacement ($\Delta x$)
